This site is where I keep notes on the AI agents and products I build: what they
do, how they work, and the decisions behind them.

I’m most interested in practical agent runtimes, multi-agent systems, AI products,
and knowledge work—especially the infrastructure that lets solo builders put agent
systems into production.

Right now, I’m building [Open Dynamic Workflows](/projects/open-dynamic-workflows/),
a runtime for portable AI-agent workflows. That is where many of the first notes
will begin.
